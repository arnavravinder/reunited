How to find, claim, and collect lost items at school — from the website to the Lost & Found desk.

How Reunited works

Reunited is the school's lost and found, online. Items handed in at the Lost & Found desk are photographed and listed on the site, where anyone can search for them.

  1. Search for the item you lost — by name, type, location, or the date you lost it.
  2. Claim it by describing details only the owner would know.
  3. Collect it from the Lost & Found desk using the claim code you receive.

Every completed claim is also listed on the public Claim Log page, so the process stays transparent.

Signing in

You need an account to claim items. There are no passwords to remember — sign in either way:

  • Continue with Google — one click with your school Google account.
  • Magic link — enter your email address and we send you a sign-in link. Open it and you're in.

Both methods lead to the same account as long as you use the same email address.

Finding your item

On the Search page, describe what you lost. You can combine an item name with filters for type, location, and the date it went missing.

Search is AI-assisted: a description like "blue bottle with a panda sticker" is matched against item descriptions, not just titles, so nearby matches surface even if the wording differs.

Results show a photo, where the item was found, and when. Open any result to see its full details.

Claiming and collecting

Found your item? Press Claim and describe features only the owner would know — markings, contents, scratches. Staff review this before approving the claim.

  1. Submit the claim with your verification details.
  2. You receive a claim code on screen and by email.
  3. Show the code at the Lost & Found desk to collect your item.

Please collect within the pickup window mentioned in your email — we send a reminder before the deadline. If an item isn't collected in time, it returns to the shelves and becomes claimable again.

Your dashboard

The Dashboard keeps everything about your account in one place:

  • Notifications — claim approvals, review updates, and pickup reminders.
  • My Claims — every claim you've made, with its current status and details.
  • Settings — update your name and choose whether to receive email notifications.

Reporting lost items yourself is coming soon; for now, items are listed by the Lost & Found desk.

Questions or problems

If a claim is taking long, a code didn't arrive, or something on the site isn't working, use the Contact form on the home page — messages go straight to the team.
